PHASE 1 • ENGINEERING PROTOTYPE

Intelligent Water Dispensing Through Touchless Automation.

AquaSense is an automatic touchless water dispenser engineered using ultrasonic sensing, Arduino-based embedded control and dual-servo mechanical actuation. The system detects a user's hand, automatically operates the water tap and closes it after a controlled dispensing cycle.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Engineering a Smarter Way to Dispense Water

Phase 1 establishes the sensing, embedded-control and mechanical actuation foundation required to automate a conventional water dispensing mechanism.

01

The Problem

Conventional water dispensers require physical contact with taps or controls. Repeated contact can reduce convenience, create hygiene concerns and result in unnecessary water flow when taps are not properly closed.

Understand the problem →
02

The Solution

AquaSense detects the presence of a hand using ultrasonic distance measurement and automatically activates a servo-controlled dispensing mechanism without requiring physical contact.

See how it works →
03

Engineering Approach

The prototype integrates electronics, embedded programming, mechanical actuation, circuit design and custom 3D-designed enclosures into one functional system.

SYSTEM WORKFLOW

From Detection to Dispensing

AquaSense follows a programmed sensing and actuation sequence controlled by the Arduino Uno.

01

Hand Enters Detection Zone

The HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor continuously measures the distance between the dispenser and nearby objects.

02

Arduino Evaluates Distance

The Arduino Uno receives the measured distance and compares it with the programmed detection threshold of 15 cm.

03

Servo Mechanism Opens the Tap

When valid detection occurs, the controller commands both servo motors to move from their closed position to the programmed open position.

04

Water Is Dispensed

The tap remains open for the programmed dispensing interval while the LCD provides system feedback to the user.

05

Tap Automatically Closes

After the dispensing period, both servos return to their closed position and the system returns to monitoring mode.
