Working Principle

Physics of sound waves and micro-actuation applied to achieve contactless operation.

Echolocation Logic

The HC-SR04 emits a 40kHz acoustic pulse. Upon striking a container, it echoes back to the receiver. Distance is computed via: Distance = (Time × Speed of Sound) / 2.

Mechanical Actuation

Once target distance is verified through software debouncing, a PWM signal commands the SG90 servo to rotate the valve arm, enabling gravity-fed fluid flow.
