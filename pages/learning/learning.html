08 — LEARNING

Engineering Insights

Key technical skills, design challenges, and takeaways from the development process.

Embedded Systems

Gained hands-on experience interfacing digital sensors (HC-SR04) with microcontroller interrupt registers, managing PWM timing constraints, and optimizing loop frequency for real-time responsiveness.

Mechatronic Design

Learned how to balance electronic power loads with mechanical actuator torque limits, mitigating physical valve friction using custom-tolerance 3D enclosure structures.

Iterative Prototyping

Understood the importance of empirical testing cycles—identifying acoustic wave deflection issues during edge-case testing and adjusting code-level delay buffers to eliminate mechanical jitter.
