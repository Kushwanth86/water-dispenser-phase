Software Architecture & Logic

Breakdown of operational loops, timing checks, and actuation triggers.

1. Ultrasonic Pulse Triggering

Initiates a clean 10-microsecond HIGH pulse on the trigger pin. Uses pulseIn() to measure echo duration and computes distance in centimeters.

2. Threshold Evaluation

If detected objects fall under the 15cm threshold, it validates container presence and switches the LCD status message.

3. Servo Actuation & Timing

Commands the SG90 servo to rotate from 0° to 90° for a controlled duration before returning to 0° to close the valve.
