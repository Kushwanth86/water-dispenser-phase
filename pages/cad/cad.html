3D Enclosure Design

Custom structural housings engineered for the AquaSense prototype.

Main Housing Unit

The primary housing unit acts as the structural chassis for the prototype. It was designed to securely mount the Arduino Uno and isolate the electrical components from the fluid dispensing area. The walls are reinforced to withstand the torque generated by the SG90 servo motor.

Protective Lid

The top lid is designed for a friction-fit closure, allowing easy access for debugging, battery replacement, and wiring adjustments without requiring mechanical fasteners. It includes precise cutouts for the 16x2 LCD interface.
