09 Project Development Timeline
09 — Team Journey

Development Timeline

Chronological progression of project milestones from conceptualization to prototype validation.

Phase A: Conceptualization

Initial problem definition, targeting touchless hygiene, and outlining basic block requirements.

Phase B: Component Selection & Breadboarding

Sourcing the Arduino Uno, ultrasonic sensor, servo, and assembling the first test circuit.

Phase C: Firmware & CAD Modeling

Writing the C++ sensor loop logic and modeling the custom enclosure box in CAD software.

Phase D: Integration & Testing

Assembling electronics inside the 3D printed enclosure and recording performance metrics.
