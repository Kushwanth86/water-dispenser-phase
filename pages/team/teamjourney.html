09 — TEAM

Development Timeline

From initial concept formulation to a functional Phase 1 prototype.

01

Idea & Concept Definition

Identified cross-contamination issues in traditional public dispensers and conceptualized a touchless ultrasonic-activated valve mechanism.

02

Component Selection

Evaluated microcontrollers and settled on the Arduino Uno combined with the HC-SR04 sensor and an SG90 servo motor for reliable actuation.

03

Circuit & Software Architecture

Designed the wiring schematic and drafted the core C++ control logic loops for distance calculation and PWM timing.

04

CAD Modeling & 3D Printing

Designed custom enclosures (main box and lid) to house the electronics securely while maintaining a clean external profile.

05

Prototype Assembly & Testing

Integrated all hardware components, ran empirical multi-cycle stress tests, documented system limits, and planned Phase 2 enhancements.
