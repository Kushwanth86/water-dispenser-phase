DESIGN PURPOSE

Why AquaSense Uses Custom CAD Parts

A functional prototype requires more than electronic components. Mechanical design transforms the circuit and control system into an organized physical product.

ENC

Component Housing

Provides dedicated physical space for electronic and mechanical components inside the prototype.

PRO

Protection

Enclosures protect internal components from accidental contact while improving overall prototype organization.

MEC

Mechanical Integration

The servo housing allows the actuation mechanism to be integrated with the automatic tap-control system.

3D

Rapid Prototyping

STL models allow the designed components to be prepared for compatible 3D-printing and fabrication workflows.

SYSTEM INTEGRATION

CAD Is One Layer of the Complete System

The AquaSense prototype combines multiple engineering layers. The hardware performs sensing and actuation, the circuit provides electrical connectivity, the Arduino software controls system behavior, and the CAD design provides the physical structure required to integrate these components.

Together, these layers transform the project from an electronic circuit into a complete engineering prototype.

FUTURE DEVELOPMENT

Mechanical Design Can Evolve

Phase 1 establishes the initial mechanical architecture. Future revisions can improve manufacturability, protection and system integration.

IP

Water Protection

Future enclosure revisions can improve resistance to water exposure around the dispensing system.

CAB

Cable Management

Dedicated cable channels and routing features can improve wiring organization.

MNT

Component Mounting

Internal mounting points can be refined for easier installation and maintenance.

ASM

Assembly Optimization

Snap fits, fasteners or modular sections can improve prototype assembly and servicing.
